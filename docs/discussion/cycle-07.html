Lessons we learnt

frontier trees are exact-optimal on every <=16-leaf window

root cause
the dominant contraction spans a window covering a constant fraction of the network; every small neighborhood is already perfect
evidence
033 systematic K=16 sweeps (~86/~182 windows, zero material gains); DP brute-force-verified; scorer-verified splices
implication
third level of local optimality for the frontier; heuristic tc chase on converged instances closed

TTF is winnable but heavy-tailed; 20%-bar claims sit near the confirmation noise floor

root cause
SA basin-entry time is a heavy-tailed random variable; worse-of-two on a single pair is high-variance; reference TTF is min-of-3-seedings, biased low
evidence
032 official TTF runs: reg3 85.4/3.7/16.1 s, sycamore 49.1/12.4/81.1 s vs ref 39.6 s
implication
future TTF work targets >=2x wins or a median-of-k arrival gate (user decision, queued)

arrival time is governed by anneal schedule, not seed quality

root cause
linear beta schedules densely cover the freezing range; DP-boosted seeds are forgotten by the time the anneal freezes
evidence
032: geometric cooling plateaus 0.1-0.2 above band; DP boost inert at n>=500 (confirms 018/006 for arrival)
implication
schedule shape and stagnation-gated diversity are the levers to port into 034/035 at scale
