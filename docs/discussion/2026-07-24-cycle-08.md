## Lessons we learnt

- **Observation**: the reference floor is beatable exactly where
  instances carry exploitable structure the anneal cannot cheaply
  discover (fusible raw gates, hyperedges, 2D-lattice regions), and by
  mechanisms that EXPLOIT structure before/around annealing rather than
  replacing the anneal. **Root cause**: tuned TreeSA is near-optimal in
  tree-rewrite space (cycles 1–7 certificates); the remaining headroom
  lives in transformations OUTSIDE that space (network preprocessing,
  seed construction in a different representation, budget allocation).
  **Evidence**: 4 records by 3 such mechanisms; 7 falsifications of
  in-space variants. *Confirmed.* **Implication**: the composite
  algorithm (route → simplify → structured seed → span-gated cold
  refinement → racing) is the right cycle-9 synthesis.
- **Observation**: pure-construction methods (separators, spectral,
  combs, mass greedy, order annealing) all fail the same way — their
  seeds lose to greedy+anneal. **Root cause**: constructions optimize a
  proxy (cut, order, width) whose floor is above what balanced
  rewrite-space search reaches; the anneal is the descent engine and
  cannot be replaced, only fed and scheduled. *Confirmed across 5
  independent attempts.* **Implication**: blacklist pure-construction
  directions on these families; innovation budget goes to acceptance
  rules, search topology (tempering), consensus reduction, and
  simplification-unlocked tree search.
- **Observation**: 13-agent concurrency distorted every local
  measurement (record-quality runs looked ordinary; OOM kills; greedy
  tie-break cascades) yet the quiet-machine scored runs recovered
  clean signals. **Implication**: the lanes+medians harness (v2.3/v2.4)
  is doing its job; local numbers are for mechanism attribution only.

  budget-fraction linear schedule (anytime-correct); racing with slope

  (~82 s pathological runs at n≈5000, 1-in-5); simplification absent
  from the default pipeline.
- Blacklist: pure-construction seeds (5 variants); congestion softmax on
  peaky profiles; cache-blocked regions on pointer trees; caterpillar/
  comb seeds (cutwidth bound); order-space annealing.
- Protocol economy: the 7 falsified attempts were not re-scored by the
  validator (their falsifications are scorer-verified locally and claim
  nothing); deviation noted.
