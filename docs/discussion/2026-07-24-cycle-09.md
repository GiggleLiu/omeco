## Lessons we learnt

- **Observation**: the composite dominates its own ingredient specialists
  (3 records in one run; every attempt-held record matched or beaten
  except one confirmation coin-flip). **Root cause**: the mechanisms are
  complementary along orthogonal axes (input transformation / seed
  representation / refinement gating / budget allocation), so composition
  adds without interference — and racing arbitrates instance-dependent
  choices (VE-as-seed, coarsened-vs-greedy arm) that no fixed router got
  right. *Confirmed.* **Implication**: the paper's algorithm artifact is
  the composite; the specialists become its ablations.
- **Observation**: global-information mechanisms that RESTRUCTURE the
  search (tempering topology, MCTS lookahead, acceptance rules) all
  failed at scale with structural causes, while global information as
  INPUT (simplification, seeds, cut evidence) keeps winning. **Implication**:
  cycle 10's waist surgery / block DP inject globality at the input/move
  level, not the search-control level — consistent with every data point.
- **Observation**: sycamore_53 record marched 60.61 → 60.17 → 60.10 →
  59.91 across three attempts with shrinking variance. **Implication**:
  the simplified-core landscape is far from exhausted; 056's exact block
  DP has a real shot at closing it with a certificate.
