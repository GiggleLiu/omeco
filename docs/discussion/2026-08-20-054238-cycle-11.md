## Lessons we learnt

1. **(top-ranked) The three mechanisms separate cleanly by *phase*, not by
   quality.** Observation: 061 wins the first 10 s (TTF 7.1 s), the
   cold ladder/059 wins matched-work refinement, plain annealing wins
   long budgets on expanders. Root cause (*confirmed* by the tc(t) curves
   and in/out-band gain accounting): early descent is bottleneck-
   negotiation (helped by local heat), late descent is plateau refinement
   (helped by cold, scale-ordered moves); a single homogeneous schedule
   cannot serve both. Evidence: 061 in-band net gain negative at 2048
   sweeps yet TTF 2.3× faster; 059 yield-rate *lower* while final tc
   better. Implication: the promoted direction is a *phase-switched
   composite* (band reheat → freeze-out front), one atomic change on 061.
2. **059 vs primaries.** Observation: big dev-bench wins, validator tie.
   Root cause (*confirmed*): reg3_250's 90 s frontier is a basin floor —
   13 mechanisms and 2 toolchains already saturate it (cycles 1–5); a
   better refiner cannot show there. Implication: matched-work refinement
   claims belong in the PR #40 ablation harness (paper), not on the
   saturated primary tc axis.
3. **060 falsification.** Observation: −0.75 on reg3. Root cause
   (*suspected*; would confirm with an activity-locality trace on reg3):
   uniform improvement density on expanders makes the worklist prune
   exactly the moves that matter; the periodic full sweeps then pay the
   pruning cost without the locality dividend. Implication: blacklist
   unconditional worklist scheduling; a locality-gated variant remains
   admissible but is not promoted.
4. *Off-goal:* the huawei dev benches (≤600 s, matched-sweep, parent
   toggle in-binary) proved a far cheaper falsification instrument than
   the 90 s validator pair — worth porting into the validator as a
   "mechanism mode" if cycle 12 is authorized.
