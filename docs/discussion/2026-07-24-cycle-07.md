## Lessons we learnt

- **Observation**: the frontier trees are exact-optimal on every ≤16-leaf
  window (033). **Root cause**: the dominant contraction sits in a window
  spanning a constant fraction of the network; every small neighborhood is
  already perfect. **Evidence**: systematic K=16 sweeps, zero material
  gains; DP validated against brute force; scorer-verified splices.
  *Confirmed.* **Implication**: strongest local-optimality statement yet
  for the frontier-convergence section — the frontier is a fixed point of
  O(1) rewrites, order-restricted DP, AND local exact re-optimization.
  Heuristic tc chase on converged instances is closed at a third level.
- **Observation**: TTF is winnable but high-variance. 032 confirmed
  16.1 s on reg3 (−20% vs reference); on sycamore its three official runs
  spread 12.4–81.1 s around the 39.6 s reference. **Root cause**:
  basin-entry time of SA is a heavy-tailed random variable; a pipeline
  whose MEDIAN arrival beats the reference can still fail worse-of-two on
  any given pair. The reference's own TTF is a min-of-3-seeding, biased
  low. **Evidence**: reports 032/{report,report2,report3}.json; local 4/4
  band crossings ≤15 s vs scored 85.4 s first run. *Confirmed.*
  **Implication**: TTF claims at the 20% bar are near the noise floor of
  single-pair confirmation; next TTF work should either target ≥2×
  improvements (far from noise) or the gate should move to median-of-k
  arrival — a user decision, queued behind the scale cycle.
- **Observation**: the pre-registered DP boost is inert as a TTF lever on
  the hard instance (032, confirming 018/006 for arrival as well as final
  tc); the real levers were schedule shape (linear beta), size-scaled
  re-heats, and stagnation-gated diversity. **Implication**: arrival time
  is governed by the anneal schedule, not by seed quality — directly
  informs 034/035 designs at scale.
