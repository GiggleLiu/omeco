## Lessons we learnt

1. **(top) Switch-point family-dependence is real; the waist-stall
   signal is not the way to find it.** Observation: immediate switch is
   optimal on sycamore (record), late on reg3 (regression); the stall
   trigger fires immediately everywhere. Root cause (*confirmed*): under
   band heat the max node cost never improves within a √n window — the
   band works by dropping sc (065), not by monotone waist-cost descent,
   so a max-node-cost stall is always "true". Evidence: waist_gain
   0.0000 on all 15 bench runs; 065's sc-led descent table. Implication:
   an adaptive switch should watch the SC TRAJECTORY (fire when sc stops
   dropping), the quantity 065 showed actually moves.
2. **Phases compose synergistically, not just sequentially.**
   Observation: 062's band prefix improves the 059 front's final tc by
   ~1 bit on ksg. Root cause (*suspected*; 065's mechanism is the best
   explanation): the reheat phase hands the front a lower-rank
   bottleneck basin to refine. Implication: the composite is the
   campaign's best general-purpose schedule; promote it to the paper.
3. **The anytime record protocol worked exactly as designed.**
   061's sycamore claim (lottery) was rejected, 063 diagnosed it, 064's
   legitimate version confirmed. The confirm-twice/worse-of-two gate
   plus pre-registered falsifications separated artifact from mechanism
   in two attempts.
