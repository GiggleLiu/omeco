## Lessons we learnt

- **Observation**: the sycamore frontier is width-optimal, yet 8.5 log2 of
  tc sits above the width bound; on reg3 the analogous overhead is ~6.
  **Root cause**: tc is a soft-max over ~n contractions; at the
  width-optimal frontier the objective is governed by the PROFILE (how
  many contractions are near-maximal), which no existing bound or
  optimizer targets — width machinery is exhausted exactly at the point
  where the profile becomes the whole game. **Evidence**: 022's cut lemma
  and cap analysis; 61.544 − 53 = 8.5 vs log2(561) = 9.1; twelve-method
  plateau. *Confirmed.* **Implication**: cycle 5's program — sum-form
  (isoperimetric-profile) lower bound + profile-aware search objective —
  is the only identified direction with headroom on these instances.
- **Observation**: both hierarchical-exact drafts nulled with the same
  signature. **Root cause**: any connected cluster of ~30–45 tensors in
  these graphs is itself a hard subproblem whose internal contraction
  spends the cost the exact top level was meant to save; coarsening
  destroys optimality before exactness can restore it. **Evidence**: 024's
  block trees at tc ≈ 130 with sc ≈ 110 regardless of geometry; 023's
  0.4–2.3 excess across all coarsenings. *Confirmed.* **Implication**:
  blacklist coarsen-then-exact on dense-width instances; exactness helps
  only below ~15 tensors (as in Staudt's glue), already standard practice.
- **Observation**: certified LBs are far below structural reality on
  expanders (18 vs ~34). **Root cause**: certifying high treewidth is
  co-NP-hard; minor-based LB search stalls on ~4-regular line graphs — a
  known tooling frontier, not a fact about the instance. **Evidence**:
  021's plateauing minor sequence; UB 34 from existing trees. *Confirmed.*
  **Implication**: for the paper, reg3 near-optimality must be argued via
  the sum-form bound (cycle 5) or stated as structural-not-certified;
  don't oversell the 18.
- **Observation**: 10× budget moved nothing at n=250/561. **Root cause**:
  SA converges to the width-optimal set well within 90 s at these sizes;
  the plateau is a property of the objective landscape, not the schedule.
  *Confirmed.* **Implication**: larger instances (cycle 5's reg3_1000,
  rqc_97_m24) are the honest arena for optimizer differentiation.
